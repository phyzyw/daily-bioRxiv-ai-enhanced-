Comparison with our previous work and the PME-CpHMD simulations in GROMACS with the CHARMM c36m force field demonstrated the robustness and sampling efficiency of pH-REX PME-CpHMD simulations. The new implementation provides a foundation for routine all-atom constant-pH simulations in Amber. The analysis highlights the need for continued development of protein force fields to more accurately balance intramolecular electrostatics and solvation of charged residues.
