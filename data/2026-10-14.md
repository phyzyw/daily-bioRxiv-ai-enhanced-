Abstract: High-grade glioma is an incurable brain cancer with a median survival of approximately 14 months. These results establish that pathological neural-tumor interactions can be harnessed to monitor glioma progression in vivo. Coupling this monitoring capability with therapeutic electrical stimulation in the same device could open up a new class of implantable, closed-loop neurotechnologies with the potential to transform glioma treatment.
