Abstract: Background Characterizing intratumor heterogeneity and subclonal architecture from bulk DNA sequencing provides important insights into cancer evolution. The CliPP-on-Web app allows non-computational researchers to directly obtain subclonal annotations of their tumor samples. Conclusions Machine learning assisted CliPP combines competitive reconstruction accuracy, substantial computational efficiency, and standardized quality control to enable reproducible subclonal reconstruction at large scale.
